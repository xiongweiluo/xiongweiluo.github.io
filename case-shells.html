Aerial Robotic Shells · Team workshop · Digital Futures / Project 04

Lift & Link

Exploring robotic assembly through discrete modules and connections.

Role
Workshop team member
Context
Digital Futures · Project 04
Output
Shell & handling prototypes
Tools & methods
Discrete geometry · Connection studies · Aerial handling · Physical prototyping

01Start with the unit and its interfaces.

Each framed module has a central handling interface and connections around its perimeter. Their relationship links the robot’s operation to the shell’s geometry.

  1. Handling interface
  2. Module frame
  3. Neighbouring connections

02Make the connection repeatable.

The workshop moved from loose components to a connected frame. Fabricating the module made the fitting and assembly sequence tangible.

03Bring the robot into the design.

Aerial handling adds a second interface: the module must be carried before it can join the shell. Workshop photographs document suspended handling and positioning beside the prototype.

04Connect individual units into a continuous shell.

The saddle-shaped model shows how discrete connections accumulate into a larger form. The workshop produced a physical shell prototype and module-handling studies.
